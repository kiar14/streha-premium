# Surface brief: Home (demo)

Scope: the demo home page (A3 prototype). Visitor mode: **Persuade**.
Audience/job: a worried homeowner on a phone who wants a roofer they can trust to keep the house dry; the action is **Pošlji povpraševanje** (enquiry form), with phone and WhatsApp secondary.
Proof: the signed 10-year watertightness warranty, 20+ years, 150 projects, a crew of 12, free site visit and quote, Slovenia and Austria, and 5 real job photos. No stars and no testimonials.
Memorable moment: the hero video: the roof builds itself on page load, then the promise and the warranty stamp appear.
Unresolved: opening hours, project locations, Stefan's portrait, registration numbers, warranty PDF.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is Stefan's site-visit survey sheet: it measures your roof and signs for it. It refuses the category default of a sunset roofer hero, stat tiles and a testimonial slider.
OWN-WORLD: Anthracite roof-slate grounds (#23262B / #1B1D21) alternate with zinc-white bands (#F2F3F1). Crisp 1px white or graphite linework, dimension lines with end ticks, pitch-angle arcs, and measuring-tape tick rows. The single accent is the roofer's snapped RED CHALK LINE and the round red warranty stamp in logo red #FF3131. Type is condensed-technical for display and a sober humanist sans for text. Square corners, and labels set like survey annotations.
STORY: The visitor understands the promise (dry, in writing, for 10 years), believes it through the certificate, the process and real roofs, and sends an enquiry through a form that reads as the survey itself.
FIRST VIEWPORT (v2, approved by user): the full-bleed hero video of the roof building itself (rafters → membrane → battens → tiles, 6 s) plays once on load. On end it crossfades into the sharp finished-roof still with a slow pull-back; a shade fades in bottom-left, the H1 "Streha, ki ostane suha. / Pisno, 10 let." reveals line by line, then the supporting line, the red [Pošlji povpraševanje] with the phone link, and the red "10 LET VODOTESNO" stamp lands top-right in the sky. The header stays visible throughout; a Preskoči pill and a red progress line show during the intro. On phones the video is a 16:10 box and the text sits below it.
FORM: Izmera (roofer's survey sheet), 7th of 7 on my ordered list; seed key 41f4d571 (degraded roll: no challengers). Signature: pinned roof-build frame sequence.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
