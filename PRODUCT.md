## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated to the client-website workflow: Next.js 16 + TypeScript + Tailwind v4 + GSAP/Lenis/Motion (premium-web-stack), deployed on Vercel. The demo (prototype) phase has no database and no real form sending.

## Users

- **Homeowners in Slovenia (and Austria)** whose roof leaks, is old, or got storm damage, or who are building new. They usually arrive on a phone after a Google search or a referral, often worried (a leak, a quote they don't trust, a roofer who never showed up), and want to know quickly: can these people be trusted, will the roof stay dry, and how do I get someone out to look.
- Secondary: small builders and investors who need roofing, sheet metal or flat-roof waterproofing on a new build.

## Product Purpose

The website for **Streha Premium d.o.o.**, a Ljubljana-based family roofing company working across Slovenia and Austria. It exists to turn visits into **enquiries ("Pošlji povpraševanje")** with enough detail about the roof (location, type, size, pitch, renovation or new, sheet-metal work, budget) that Streha Premium can come for a free site visit and send a quote. Success means more qualified enquiries, and a first impression that is more trustworthy than the competitors' bought-template sites.

## Positioning

- **A written 10-year watertightness warranty on every job**, on a certificate signed by director Stefan Gabor. No Ljubljana competitor shows a specific, signed warranty.
- It comes with a **free site visit and quote**, work that **starts right after the visit**, and one crew for everything on a roof: tiles, sheet metal, waterproofing, flat roofs and gutters.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof over adjectives.** Every trust claim points to something real: the signed warranty, real roofs, named brands, the process.
2. **Watertight is the promise.** The site sells a dry house for the next decade, not "roofing services".
3. **One tap to an enquiry.** "Pošlji povpraševanje" is visible everywhere, with phone and WhatsApp as the quick alternatives.
4. **No big lies.** Vivid framing is fine; invented numbers, reviews or certifications are not.
5. **Mobile first.** Most visitors are worried homeowners on a phone.
