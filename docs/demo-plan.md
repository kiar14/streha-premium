### How the hero video plays (details)
- It **plays automatically once** on page load (muted and inline, so phones allow autoplay). There are no controls, and it doesn't loop.
- A **poster image** (the first frame, `hero-01`) shows instantly, so there is never a black box while the video loads.
- **After the video ends, it holds the last frame** (the finished roof), and the text and buttons animate in over about 1.2 s.
- **Visitors with "reduce motion" turned on, or on slow connections,** get the finished-roof still with the text straight away.
- **If they come back in the same visit,** the text is already there (the video doesn't replay).
- **Mobile:** the video is 4:3, so full-screen portrait would cut off the sides of the house. On phones, the video fills the **top part of the screen** (full width) and the text appears **under it** after the video ends.
- **Size:** compressed to about **1.5–2.5 MB** (MP4 + WebM), with a smaller version for phones. I do the compression with ffmpeg; you only deliver the raw video.
