# Demo plan – Streha Premium d.o.o.

**Step:** A2 · Demo plan · 27 Sep 2026
**Built from:** `docs/research.md` and `PRODUCT.md`. The pictures are listed in `docs/asset-plan.md`.

## Decisions
| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Primary action | **"Pošlji povpraševanje"** button → enquiry form, everywhere. Phone and WhatsApp are secondary. |
| Lead promise | **10 let pisne garancije na vodotesnost** |
| Visual direction | **Izmera – the survey sheet.** The site reads like Stefan's site-visit sheet: anthracite roof-slate grounds, crisp white linework with dimension lines and pitch angles, and the roofer's **red chalk line** (logo red `#FF3131`) as the only accent. The logo's red sun becomes the round **"10 let vodotesno"** warranty stamp. |
| Signature moment | **"Streha nastaja"**: a photoreal roof builds itself layer by layer on scroll and ends in rain running off. |
| Proof rules | Use 150 projektov and 12 sodelavcev. **No stars, no review counts, no testimonials.** Use vivid but true framings (10 blagovnih znamk, Slovenija in Avstrija, brezplačen ogled). |
| Language | Slovenian, formal "vi", calm and direct (no exclamation marks) |

## Navigation
Logo (left) · **Storitve · Kako delamo · Garancija · Projekti · O nas** · phone `041 815 559` · **[Pošlji povpraševanje]** (red button)
- **Mobile:** logo plus a menu button at the top. At the bottom, a **sticky action bar**: `Pošlji povpraševanje` (wide) · call icon · WhatsApp icon. It replaces today's popup chat widget.
