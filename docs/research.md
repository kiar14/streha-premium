## 2. What they stand for

From the snippets of their own copy:
- **Headline / promise:** "Strokovne krovske storitve, ki jim lahko zaupate" (*Professional roofing services you can trust*)
- **Tagline:** "Krovstvo – vse za streho" (*Roofing – everything for your roof*)
- **Positioning:** a *family business* with a *team of experts* "dedicated to the best service and quality", *one company for everything to do with the roof*, *no waiting* for repairs and replacements, *fast help after storms*.
- **Tone of voice:** formal Slovenian, generic trade-directory style ("strokovno", "kakovostno", "zaupanje"). Warm but not personal: no names, faces or stories.

**What makes them different (claimed):** full service (roofing + sheet metal + carpentry + waterproofing), emergency and storm response, "no waiting", a wide choice of roofing brands.

**Honest assessment:** none of these is unique yet. Competitors say the same things (see section 6). The differentiator has to come from **proof**: real projects, a named owner, a clear written warranty, and response times.

**Important for the new site.** The homepage title and copy are word-for-word the same as **streha-expert.si** (the same company's previous name), and **popravilostrehe.si** lists the identical brand list. The current site looks like a shared template. That means:
- There's no distinctive brand to preserve. The new site can set the brand properly.
- Duplicate text on several domains hurts SEO. Ask the client which domains they own and plan 301 redirects from streha-expert.si (and any others) to the new site.

## 3. Vibe – the personality the new site should have

1. **Trustworthy** – they ask people to trust them with the most expensive part of a house, and the business record is short, so visible proof (owner, warranty, real roofs) has to carry the page.
2. **Crafted** – roofing is precision work with metal, clay and timber. Clean detail, honest materials and close-up textures show skill better than stock "happy family" photos.
3. **Responsive** – storm call-outs and "no waiting" are their strongest service claims. The site should feel quick: a phone number always in reach and short paths to "call" or "request a quote".

Name to live up to: "**Premium**" means a calm, quality look (restraint, good type, real photography), not flashy luxury.

## 5. What customers care about

The company's own reviews could not be read (2 on mojmojster). Below is what Slovenian homeowners ask and complain about, from forums (slo-tech, podsvojostreho.net), press (Žurnal24, Večer) and competitor listings:

**What people ask before hiring**
- **Is the quoted price final?** Forum advice is to have it written in the quote and contract that the price is final and the roof will meet all regulations. → *Fixed written quote*
- **Which roofing suits my roof?** Slope, climate, weight. A heavier roof may need the timber structure reinforced. → *Advice before the quote*
- **Do I need a building permit?** Žurnal24: "in most cases a permit is needed" for a roof replacement. → *They should explain or handle the paperwork*
- **How is it built underneath?** Membrane, ventilation gap, battens, so that a leak can't reach the house. → *Show the layers and the craft*
- **How fast can you come?** Emergency leaks and storm damage. → *Response time*
- **Warranty** on workmanship and materials.

**What people praise (in competitors' reviews)**
- Expertise and experience, reliability, professionalism, a tidy and precise job, keeping to the agreed price and date.

**What people complain about**
- Contractors misleading clients on price or scope (a podsvojostreho thread titled "streha, zavajanje izvajalca"), unfinished or leaking work, and not knowing whom to complain to (a thread asking which inspection body checks roofers' work).

**Market benchmark:** Roofers on Mojmojster average **8.6 / 10** (≈1,700 reviews), and sheet-metal roofing specialists average **9.5 / 10**.

**→ Trust bar candidates** (only once the client confirms each one):
1. Fixed written quote, free site visit
2. Warranty: X years on workmanship (**MISSING**)
3. Response time for emergencies and storms, e.g. "within 24 h" (**MISSING**)
4. Owner's years of experience, stated truthfully (**MISSING**)
5. Brands they install (Tondach, Creaton, Bramac, Trimo…)
