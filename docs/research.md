## 2. What they stand for

- **Promise:** "Strokovne krovske storitve, ki jim lahko zaupate" (*Roofing services you can trust*) and "Krovstvo – vse za streho" (*everything for the roof*).
- **Values in their words:** strokovnost (expertise), kakovost (quality), zanesljivost (reliability), hitrost / "brez čakanja" (speed, no waiting), prijaznost (friendly crew), družinsko podjetje (family business).
- **What makes them different:**
  - They start right after the site visit.
  - A **10-year watertightness warranty on every job**, in writing and signed. This is their strongest proof point, and competitors don't show anything like it.
  - Everything from one firm: tiles, sheet metal, waterproofing, flat roofs, gutters.
  - Nationwide coverage.
  - A named owner who is on the roof ("Stefan in ekipa…").
- **Tone of voice:** direct, energetic, lots of exclamation marks, "Pokličite in rezervirajte svoj termin!". Formal "vi". The new site should keep the directness but lose the shouting: calm confidence.

## 3. Vibe – the personality of the new site

1. **Zanesljiv / Dependable.** The written 10-year warranty and "we start right away" are promises about reliability, and the site must feel solid and plain-spoken, like a signed contract.
2. **Mojstrski / Crafted.** Roofing is layers of membrane, battens, tiles and flashing done precisely. Close-up material textures and real job photos show the craft better than smiling stock faces.
3. **Odziven / Responsive.** "Brez čakanja", storm call-outs and WhatsApp mean the phone must be one tap away everywhere, and the site should move quickly and crisply.

"Premium" should come through as calm, quality restraint (dark slate, clean type, real texture), not flashy luxury.

## 5. What customers care about

The only reviews available are the 3 site testimonials, which praise a very professional crew, great effort, a great-looking roof, friendly people, keeping to the agreed schedule, and "Stefan did excellent work". The market-wide picture comes from forums (slo-tech, podsvojostreho.net), press (Žurnal24, Večer) and competitor reviews.

**Praised:** expertise, a tidy and precise job, keeping to the price and date, a friendly crew, the owner being on site.

**Complained about:**
- Contractors changing the price or scope after the quote ("zavajanje izvajalca")
- Leaks after the job with no one taking responsibility
- Waiting weeks or months for a roofer
- Not knowing whom to complain to

**Asked before hiring:**
- Is the price final and in writing?
- Which roofing suits my roof's pitch and structure?
- Does the timber structure need reinforcing?
- Do I need a building permit? (Žurnal24: usually yes for a replacement)
- How soon can you start?
- What warranty do I get?

**Market benchmark:** roofers on Mojmojster average 8.6/10 (≈1,700 reviews).

### → Trust bar (for A2)
1. **10 let garancije na vodotesnost** (verified, certificate)
2. **20+ let izkušenj** (confirmed by user)
3. **Začnemo takoj po ogledu** (their promise; confirm the typical time from visit to start)
4. **Pisna ponudba, jasna cena** (their process step 2; confirm "fixed price")
5. **Po vsej Sloveniji** (verified)
6. *Optional:* 4.8 ★ from 44 reviews, **only if the source is confirmed**
